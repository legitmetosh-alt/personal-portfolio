01 | ABOUT ME

A little about me.

My schooling started in Nyeri and has brought me to KCA University.

I started kindergarten in Nyeri, then joined Nyeri Good Shepherd International Academy for Grade One. After that I went to Kagumo High School, and now I'm studying Computer Science at KCA University.

Outside class, I'm interested in cybersecurity, especially how technology can protect people and businesses. I also like finance, investing and quantitative trading. I enjoy working with numbers and making decisions based on evidence.

When I'm not studying, you'll find me debating ideas, following Formula 1 and football, playing chess, or reading about business, entrepreneurship, leadership and public affairs. I'm ambitious and curious, and I'm always looking for ways to turn what I learn into something practical.
